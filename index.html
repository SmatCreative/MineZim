Built for Zimbabwe Mining

Compliant, secure, and industry-specific from the ground up

Verified Accounts

Every user is verified against their account type. Companies submit CR14 + ITF263. Miners submit AGML or Mining Location certificates. Explosives access requires ZRP clearance and Chief Inspector permit.

Compliance Gates

Controlled substance listings are hidden from unverified users. Attempting to contact a seller of explosives or cyanide without the correct license on file is blocked and flagged automatically.

Regulatory Framework

All listings enforced under the Mines and Minerals Act [Chapter 21:05], Explosives Act [Chapter 10:08], EMA Act [Chapter 20:27], and ZIMRA regulations. MMCZ oversight on all mineral transactions.
